## Brand

**Klassi** `/klas-si/`

Derived from **"Classy"** (elegant, professional) + **"Class"** (lớp học).

> *"Lớp học đẳng cấp"* — Your class, elevated.

The name carries two layers of meaning simultaneously: the core product (class management) and the promise (professional, high-quality teaching). It works in both Vietnamese and English, is short enough for a memorable domain, and has no existing competitor using it in the VN market.

**Taglines:**
- Vietnamese: *"Dạy học chuyên nghiệp hơn"*
- English: *"Your class, elevated"*

**Sub-products:**
- **Klassi ClassHub** — B2B SaaS management suite for teachers
- **Klassi TutorMatch** — B2C marketplace connecting parents and tutors

---

## What is Klassi?

**Hệ thống giúp giáo viên dạy chuyên nghiệp hơn, thu tiền gọn hơn, chốt học viên dễ hơn.**

A hybrid B2B + B2C education platform for the Vietnamese market, built for individual tutors and small tutoring centers — offline, online, and hybrid.

| Module | Type | Strategy |
|--------|------|----------|
| **Klassi ClassHub** | B2B SaaS | **Build first** — clear pain, clear revenue, clear upgrade path |
| **Klassi TutorMatch** | B2C Marketplace | **Open after ClassHub traction** — avoid cold-start two sides at once |

**Core approach:** Tool-first, marketplace-after.
- Free landing page as entry point → teacher gets immediate value, Klassi gets organic exposure
- ClassHub earns revenue → seeds TutorMatch supply naturally
- TutorMatch opens in stages: profiles → search → booking → boost

## Core Insight

> Vietnamese teachers with 3+ classes are drowning in Zalo groups, Excel sheets, and cash collection headaches.  
> Parents pay millions per month for tutoring but find tutors through untrusted Facebook groups with zero verification.  
> **EduConnect** solves both sides — and the two modules create a natural flywheel.

---

## The Flywheel

```
TutorMatch → Teacher gets students
           → Teacher uses ClassHub to manage them
           → Parents see teacher is professional
           → Parents leave verified reviews
           → Reviews boost teacher in TutorMatch
           → More students → teacher upgrades plan
           → Platform earns from subscriptions + transaction fees + boosts
```
